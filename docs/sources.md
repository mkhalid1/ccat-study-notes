## What is not published

We looked for these on Criteria's pages and did not find them. The notes say so wherever they come up:

- a pass mark or the score ranges a given employer uses
- the full raw-score-to-percentile table
- how many questions of each type appear, and in what order
- whether you can go back to an earlier question
- whether scratch paper is allowed
- a general retake policy

## What is our own guidance

These parts are our study advice, not Criteria statements, and are labelled as guidance where they appear:

- the per-type timing targets and clock checkpoints
- the "30 or more on practice" target
- the step-by-step methods, traps lists and study plans
- all practice items and explanations, which are original
