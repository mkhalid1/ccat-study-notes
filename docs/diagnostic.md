# 15-minute diagnostic

Fifteen original items, three from each question type, to show you where to start. Set a timer for **5 minutes**, which is the CCAT's real pace of 18 seconds a question, and answer everything without a calculator. Then check your answers, read the explanations, and use the table at the bottom. With the review, the whole thing takes about 15 minutes.

These items are original, written for these notes. They are not taken from the CCAT or from Criteria's materials.

If you already know the question types, skip this and take the [free full-length CCAT practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ccat?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ccat) instead. A full 50-question run gives a better picture of pacing.

## Part A: Verbal

**D1.** Which word is most nearly OPPOSITE to FRUGAL?

A. thrifty · B. wasteful · C. poor · D. careful · E. simple

**D2.** BOOK is to LIBRARY as PAINTING is to:

A. frame · B. gallery · C. artist · D. canvas · E. brush

**D3.** Which word is CLOSEST in meaning to OBSOLETE?

A. modern · B. outdated · C. broken · D. rare · E. costly

## Part B: Math word problems

**D4.** What is 30% of 250?

A. 25 · B. 70 · C. 75 · D. 80 · E. 175

**D5.** A train leaves at 9:40 a.m. and arrives at 12:15 p.m. How long is the journey?

A. 2 h 25 min · B. 2 h 35 min · C. 2 h 45 min · D. 3 h 25 min · E. 3 h 35 min

**D6.** The average of six numbers is 9. One of the numbers, 14, is removed. What is the average of the remaining five?

A. 7 · B. 7.5 · C. 8 · D. 8.5 · E. 9

## Part C: Number and letter series

**D7.** 7, 12, 17, 22, ?

A. 24 · B. 25 · C. 26 · D. 27 · E. 29

**D8.** 3, 6, 12, 24, ?

A. 30 · B. 36 · C. 42 · D. 48 · E. 54

**D9.** 1, 2, 4, 7, 11, ?

A. 14 · B. 15 · C. 16 · D. 17 · E. 22

## Part D: Logic

**D10.** All pilots passed the medical. Jo passed the medical. *Jo is a pilot.* True, false or uncertain?

**D11.** Kim is older than Lou. Lou is older than Max. *Kim is older than Max.* True, false or uncertain?

**D12.** If the day before yesterday was Monday, what day is tomorrow?

A. Tuesday · B. Wednesday · C. Thursday · D. Friday · E. Saturday

## Part E: Spatial reasoning

**D13.** Which figure does not belong?

```
A. ↑      B. →      C. ↓      D. ←      E. ↔
```

**D14.** The shading turns the same way each step. What comes next?

```
◐      ◓      ◑      ◒      ?
```

A. ◐ · B. ◓ · C. ◑ · D. ◒ · E. ●

**D15.** Each row follows the same rule. Which figure goes in the empty cell?

```
○   ◑   ●
◑   ●   ○
●   ○   ?
```

A. ○ · B. ◑ · C. ● · D. ◆ · E. □

<details>
<summary>Answers and explanations</summary>

**D1: B, wasteful.** Frugal means careful not to waste money or resources. *Why not A:* "thrifty" is a synonym.

**D2: B, gallery.** A library is a place where books are kept and shown to the public. A gallery is the same for paintings. *Why not A or D:* a frame and a canvas are parts of a painting, not where it is kept.

**D3: B, outdated.** Obsolete means no longer used because something newer has replaced it. *Why not C:* an obsolete machine may still work perfectly.

**D4: C, 75.** 10% of 250 is 25, so 30% is 75. *Why not E:* 175 is what is left after taking 30% away.

**D5: B, 2 h 35 min.** 9:40 to 11:40 is 2 hours, 11:40 to 12:00 is 20 minutes, and 12:00 to 12:15 is 15 more: 2 hours 35 minutes.

**D6: C, 8.** Six numbers with an average of 9 add up to 54. Removing 14 leaves 40, and 40 ÷ 5 = 8.

**D7: D, 27.** Add 5 each time.

**D8: D, 48.** Double each time.

**D9: C, 16.** The gaps are 1, 2, 3, 4, so the next gap is 5: 11 + 5 = 16.

**D10: Uncertain.** All pilots passed, but others may have passed too. Passing does not make Jo a pilot.

**D11: True.** In order of age: Kim, Lou, Max.

**D12: C, Thursday.** The day before yesterday was Monday, so yesterday was Tuesday, today is Wednesday and tomorrow is Thursday.

**D13: E.** Every other figure is a single arrow pointing one way. E points both ways.

**D14: A, ◐.** The dark half moves a quarter turn clockwise each step: left, top, right, bottom. The next is left again.

**D15: B, ◑.** Each row has one empty, one half-filled and one full circle. The third row has a full and an empty circle, so the missing figure is the half-filled circle. The columns follow the same rule, which confirms it.

</details>

## What your result means

Count your correct answers in each part. If you ran out of time, count unanswered items as wrong: that is how the real test scores them.

| Part | Your score | If you scored 0 or 1 | If you scored 2 | If you scored 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Verbal | /3 | Start with the [verbal guide](sections/verbal.md) | Do its practice items and check the instruction word every time | Light review only |
| B. Math | /3 | Start with the [math guide](sections/math-word-problems.md) | Learn the mental math table | Light review only |
| C. Series | /3 | Start with the [series guide](sections/number-series.md) | Learn the method order | Light review only |
| D. Logic | /3 | Start with the [logic guide](sections/logic.md) | Learn the patterns table | Light review only |
| E. Spatial | /3 | Start with the [spatial guide](sections/spatial-reasoning.md) | Practise the feature list | Light review only |

**Did you finish in 5 minutes?** If not, timing is your biggest lever, whatever your part scores. Read the [timing guide](timing.md) early.

Then pick a plan: the [7-day plan](study-plans.md#7-day-plan) if you have a week, or the [2-day plan](study-plans.md#2-day-plan) if the test is close.
