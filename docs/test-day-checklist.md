# Test-day checklist

Fifteen minutes goes fast. Most of what decides your score happens before you click start: the room, the device and the plan in your head.

## The day before

- [ ] Read your invitation email again. Note the deadline, the link and anything it says about the test, devices or time.
- [ ] Choose your device. Criteria says its tests work on laptops, desktops, tablets and phones, unless the instructions say otherwise. A laptop or desktop with a mouse is usually easiest for fast clicking.
- [ ] Check your browser. Criteria recommends Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari, with cookies and JavaScript turned on.
- [ ] Do one short timed set, then stop. No cramming.
- [ ] Sleep.

## One hour before

- [ ] Find a quiet room where nobody will interrupt you for 20 minutes. Criteria suggests moving kids, pets or housemates elsewhere, or telling people you will be busy.
- [ ] Charge the device or plug it in. Use a stable internet connection.
- [ ] Close every other tab and app. Mute notifications.
- [ ] If your instructions allow scratch paper, have paper and two pens ready. Criteria's pages do not say whether it is allowed, so check your instructions.
- [ ] Keep calculators away. Criteria says no calculators are allowed.

## In the first minute

- [ ] Read the instructions in full. Criteria advises reading them carefully, and they may include timing hints.
- [ ] Note the start time on the timer, and remind yourself of the three checkpoints: question 13 by 10:00 left, 26 by 5:00 left, 37 by 1:00 left.
- [ ] Remind yourself of the one rule: no single question gets more than about 40 seconds.

## During the test

- [ ] Read the instruction word on every verbal item: opposite or same meaning?
- [ ] In math, read the question sentence before the numbers.
- [ ] In series, write the gaps first.
- [ ] In logic, draw it.
- [ ] Guess and move on when a question is stuck. Criteria says there is no penalty for wrong answers.
- [ ] Check the clock at each checkpoint, not after every question.
- [ ] In the last minute, answer every remaining question you can reach, even with a guess.

## If something goes wrong

- [ ] If the page does not load, check your connection and make sure cookies and JavaScript are on. Criteria says loading errors usually come from an old browser or unstable internet.
- [ ] If you see "Error submitting test", Criteria's FAQ says to copy your Event ID into the assessment centre, where you can submit or resume.
- [ ] If you see "You cannot restart this event again", contact Criteria Support using the chat button in the bottom right of the assessment centre.
- [ ] If ads appear over the test, Criteria says that usually means malware on the device. Switch to a different device.

## Afterwards

- [ ] Your result goes to the employer, not to you. Questions about your result or your application go to the employer, not to Criteria.
- [ ] Write down what felt hardest while you remember it. If you sit another aptitude test later, start your practice there.

## Last practice before the real one

If you have time for one more full run, take the [free timed CCAT practice test on PrepClubs](https://prepclubs.com/free-practice-tests/ccat?utm_source=github&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=study-notes&utm_content=ccat) a day or two before, not on the morning itself.
